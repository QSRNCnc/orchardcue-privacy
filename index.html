Privacy Policy

OrchardCue · Last updated: November 7, 2026

OrchardCue is an offline organizer for household remote profiles, brand/protocol indexes, and scene plans. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not.

1. Summary

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Remote names, protocol IDs, scenes, and activity stay on your iPhone.
  • Reference packs and the brand index are bundled with the app and used locally.
  • OrchardCue does not transmit infrared from the iPhone.

2. Data stored on device

OrchardCue may store: saved remote profiles, scene configurations, match selections, and a short local activity timeline. This information is written with Apple platform storage (UserDefaults / on-device files) and is not uploaded by the app.

3. Network

Core features work offline. OrchardCue does not require a server to browse brands, run scene plans, or use the practice pad. The in-app privacy document is loaded from the app bundle.

4. Permissions

OrchardCue does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location, Bluetooth, or tracking. The current release does not include hardware accessory integrations.

5. Children

OrchardCue is a utility for organizing household remote profiles. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Third parties

We do not sell personal data. The app does not embed third-party ad or analytics networks.

7. Data retention & deletion

You can remove saved remotes inside the app. Uninstalling OrchardCue deletes local app data subject to iOS behavior.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the in-app copy shipped with the next release.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: use App Store Connect support for OrchardCue, or open an issue on the public policy page repository:
